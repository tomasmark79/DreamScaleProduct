FAQ

Good to know.

Do I need MIDI?+

No. Choose a scale in the app and display it over USB. MIDI is only needed if you want the lights to respond to played notes.

What computer and software do I need?+

A Windows or Linux computer running the DreamScaler Python app, a USB connection and external power for the strip. The app works without music software such as Bitwig, but still needs a computer.

Will it work with my keyboard?+

You can attach the strip wherever there is a suitable surface and enough room. Assign LEDs to keys in a configuration file to match your layout. The Arturia KeyLab 49 MKII and 61-key configurations are templates you can adapt.

Does the light cover black keys too?+

Yes. The strip has 144 LEDs per metre, allowing close alignment with white and black keys. Set the matching LED positions in the configuration file.

Can I shorten the strip?+

Yes. Trim the 1-metre strip at its marked cut points, then update the configuration to match its new length and position.

Can I use it on an acoustic piano?+

Yes. Attach the strip and match its LEDs to the keys. Display scales from the app over USB without MIDI. The strip does not detect acoustic sound; reacting to played notes requires MIDI input.

Do I need Bitwig Studio?+

No. You can set scales directly in the app. Bitwig integration is optional; it follows scale changes and saves settings with your project. The video shows Bitwig playback only. We plan to redesign the standalone app.

Is this a keyboard or a light system?+

DreamScaler is an LED light system and companion software for a keyboard you already own. A keyboard and computer are not included.

Is the interactive preview the actual software?+

No. It is a browser demo of scale colors and sound. It does not connect to your hardware or show the final app interface.
