FAQ

Good to know.

Do I need MIDI?+

No. The first DreamScaler product displays scales using the companion application and a USB connection. It requires neither MIDI nor music production software.

What computer and software do I need?+

The current Python application supports Windows and Linux. DreamScaler needs a computer, USB and external power for the strip. macOS compatibility is expected but has not yet been verified. The software is being revised; final setup details will follow.

Will it work with my keyboard?+

The strip can be attached to a suitable surface and covers a maximum length of 1 metre. Full-keyboard coverage depends on the width of your keys. LED-to-key alignment is configurable for different layouts. The Arturia KeyLab 49 MKII and 61-key configurations serve as starting templates.

Does the light cover black keys too?+

Yes. The strip has 144 LEDs per metre, allowing close alignment with white and black keys through a configurable layout.

Can I shorten the strip?+

Yes. The maximum strip length is 1 metre. It can be shortened at marked cut points, with its layout adapted to the resulting length and position.

Can I use it on an acoustic piano?+

Yes. Scale display works on an acoustic piano with room for the strip. The lights show the selected scale; they do not listen to or detect the notes you play.

Do I need Bitwig Studio?+

No. The first product is focused on scale display through its own application. MIDI response through a Bitwig extension is planned for a later stage and will be offered separately.

Is this a keyboard or a light system?+

DreamScaler is an LED light system and companion software for a keyboard you already own. A keyboard and computer are not included.

Is the interactive preview the actual software?+

No. It is a browser demo of scale colors and sound. It does not connect to your hardware or show the final app interface.

When can I get one?+

The first kits are being prepared. Pricing, availability and setup instructions will be published here before orders open.
