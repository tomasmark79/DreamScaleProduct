YOUR SETUP. A NEW DIMENSION.

Your instrument.
Your layout. Your light.

A piano, a MIDI controller, or another keyboard: wherever there is a suitable surface and room for the strip, you can make it part of your setup. Custom LED-to-key mapping adapts the lights to your layout.

See the Bitwig playback demo ↗
  1. 01

    Make it fit.

    Find a suitable place to stick the strip along your keys. Shorten it to the length you need at the marked cut points, then attach it to your instrument.

  2. 02

    Map it to your keys.

    Define which LEDs belong to each white or black key in a configuration file. The existing Arturia mappings are starting templates you can adapt to your own keyboard.

  3. 03

    Bring your setup to life.

    Connect the controller by USB and the strip to its power supply. Use your configured mapping to bring scales and MIDI-driven notes to your keys. See the current Bitwig playback integration in the video.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before the
first note.

Will it work with my keyboard?+

DreamScaler is designed to adapt to your instrument. The strip can be attached to a piano, MIDI controller or another keyboard wherever there is a suitable surface and enough space. LED positions are assigned to keys in a configuration file, so you can calibrate the mapping for your own layout. The Arturia KeyLab 49 MKII and 61-key configurations are starting templates for other setups.

Does the light cover black keys too?+

Yes. With 144 LEDs per metre, the strip provides closely spaced light sources for both white and black keys. You define the LED-to-key assignments in the configuration file to match the strip's actual position on your instrument.

Can I shorten the strip?+

Yes. The supplied strip is 1 metre long and can be trimmed at its marked cut points to suit the space on your instrument. Adjust the LED-to-key mapping in the configuration file to match the shortened strip.

Can I use it on an acoustic piano?+

You can mount the strip on an acoustic piano wherever there is a suitable surface and enough room. Scale lighting can be positioned to match its keys. For lights to respond to played notes, DreamScaler needs MIDI data from a connected source; attaching the strip alone does not detect acoustic piano notes.

Do I need Bitwig Studio?+

No. The current project also includes a standalone piano tool for scales and light effects. We plan to redesign the standalone application and will present its updated interface and workflow separately. The embedded video demonstrates playback in Bitwig Studio only.

Is this a keyboard or a light system?+

DreamScaler is an LED light system and companion software for a keyboard you already own. A keyboard and computer are not included.

Is the interactive preview the actual software?+

The interactive keyboard on this page is a browser illustration of scale coloring, with synthesized sound. It does not connect to MIDI or hardware and does not represent the standalone application's interface. The video shows the real LED hardware during Bitwig Studio playback.

When can I get one?+

The first DreamScaler set is being prepared. Pricing, availability, supported systems and final setup instructions will be announced here before orders open.
